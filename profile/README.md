## 🏢 About Blockmedia
**Korea’s No.1 Blockchain Media Outlet**

We are a specialized media outlet dedicated to expanding blockchain adoption and understanding. We strive to be your trusted partner in Korea.

* 🏆 **Market Leader:** A specialized media outlet dedicated to expanding blockchain adoption and understanding.
* 📣 **Multi-Channel Engagement:** Evolved from a news portal to a comprehensive platform engaging investors via YouTube, Telegram, and X.
* 🌐 **Global Networking:** Host of "BlockFesta," a premier annual conference connecting global projects with the Korean market.
* 🚀 **Our Mission:** To build a healthy ecosystem for retail investors and Web3 projects.
